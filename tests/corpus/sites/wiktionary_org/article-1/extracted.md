### Etymology

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=example&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Etymology")\]

From [Middle English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_English "w:Middle English") _[example](#Middle_English)_, _[exaumple](/wiki/exaumple#Middle_English "exaumple")_, from [Old French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_French "w:Old French") _[example](#Old_French)_, _[essaumple](/w/index.php?title=essaumple&action=edit&redlink=1 "essaumple (page does not exist)")_, from [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "w:Latin") _[exemplum](/wiki/exemplum#Latin "exemplum")_ (“sample, pattern, specimen, copy for imitation, etc.”, literally “what is taken out”); see _[exempt](/wiki/exempt#English "exempt")_. [Doublet](/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#doublet "Appendix:Glossary") of _[exemplum](/wiki/exemplum#English "exemplum")_ and _[sample](/wiki/sample#English "sample")_. Displaced native [Middle English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_English "w:Middle English") _[forebisne](/w/index.php?title=forebisne&action=edit&redlink=1 "forebisne (page does not exist)")_, from [Old English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_English "w:Old English") _[forebȳsn](/wiki/forebysn#Old_English "forebysn")_; and [Middle English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_English "w:Middle English") _[bisne](/wiki/bisne#Middle_English "bisne")_, from [Old English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_English "w:Old English") _[bȳsn](/wiki/bysn#Old_English "bysn")_ (modern [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language "w:English language") _[bizen](/wiki/bizen#English "bizen")_).

### Noun

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=example&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Noun")\]

**example** (_plural_ **[examples](/wiki/examples#English "examples")**)

1. Something that is representative of all such things in a group.
    * **2005** May 23, Gavriel D. Rosenfeld, The World Hitler Never Made: Alternate History and the Memory of Nazism‎[\[1\]](https://books.google.co.nz/books?id=ZKNdxlzG6okC), [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press "w:Cambridge University Press"), [→ISBN](/wiki/Special:BookSources/9780521847063 "Special:BookSources/9780521847063"), page 182:
        
        If Demandt's essay served as a strident **example** of the German desire for normalcy, a more subtle **example** was provided by a brief allohistorical depiction of a Nazi victory in World War II written by German historian Michael Salewski in 1999.
        
    * **2013** July 26, [Leo Hickman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Hickman "w:Leo Hickman"), “[How algorithms rule the world](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2013/jul/01/how-algorithms-rule-world-nsa)”, in [The Guardian Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian_Weekly "w:The Guardian Weekly"), volume 189, number 7, page 26:
        
        The use of algorithms in policing is one **example** of their increasing influence on our lives. And, as their ubiquity spreads, so too does the debate around whether we should allow ourselves to become so reliant on them – and who, if anyone, is policing their use.
        
2. Something that serves to illustrate or explain a [rule](/wiki/rule "rule").
    * **2013** May-June, [David Van Tassel](http://www.americanscientist.org/authors/detail/david-van-tassel), [Lee DeHaan](http://www.americanscientist.org/authors/detail/lee-dehaan), “[Wild Plants to the Rescue](http://www.americanscientist.org/issues/feature/2013/3/wild-plants-to-the-rescue)”, in [American Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Scientist "w:American Scientist"), volume 101, number 3:
        
        Plant breeding is always a numbers game. \[…\] The wild species we use are rich in genetic variation, \[…\]. In addition, we are looking for rare alleles, so the more plants we try, the better. These rarities may be new mutations, or they can be existing ones that are neutral—or are even selected against—in a wild population. A good **example** is mutations that disrupt seed dispersal, leaving the seeds on the heads long after they are ripe.
        
3. Something that serves as a [pattern](/wiki/pattern "pattern") of [behaviour](/wiki/behaviour "behaviour") to be [imitated](/wiki/imitate "imitate") (a [good](/wiki/good "good") example) or not to be imitated (a [bad](/wiki/bad "bad") example).
    
    _Nelson Mandela was an **example** for many to follow._
    
    * **1611**, [The Holy Bible, \[…\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible "w:Bible") ([King James Version](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_James_Version "w:King James Version")), London: \[…\] [Robert Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Barker_\(printer\) "w:Robert Barker (printer)"), \[…\], [→OCLC](https://search.worldcat.org/title/964384981), [John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gospel_of_John "w:Gospel of John") [13:15](https://archive.org/details/Bible1611/page/n1337/mode/1up), column 1:
        
        For I haue giuen you an **example**, that yee ſhould doe, as I haue done to you.
        
    * **1671**, [John Milton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Milton "w:John Milton"), “[Samson Agonistes, \[…\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samson_Agonistes "w:Samson Agonistes").”, in [Paradise Regain’d. A Poem. In IV Books. To which is Added, Samson Agonistes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradise_Regained "w:Paradise Regained"), London: \[…\] J\[ohn\] M\[acock\] for John Starkey \[…\], [→OCLC](https://search.worldcat.org/title/228732398), [page 52](https://archive.org/details/paradiseregaindp00milt_0/page/52/mode/1up), lines 823–824:

    * **1897** December (indicated as **1898**), [Winston Churchill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winston_Churchill_\(novelist\) "w:Winston Churchill (novelist)"), chapter IV, in [The Celebrity: An Episode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity "w:The Celebrity"), New York, N.Y.: [The Macmillan Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macmillan_Inc. "w:Macmillan Inc."); London: [Macmillan & Co., Ltd.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macmillan_Publishers "w:Macmillan Publishers"), [→OCLC](https://search.worldcat.org/title/222716698), [pages 58–59](https://archive.org/details/celebrityanepis01churgoog/page/n76/mode/1up):
