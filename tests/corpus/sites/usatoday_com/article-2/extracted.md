# Americans don't like Trump's Jesus posts and back the Pope, poll shows

[Terry Collins](/staff/6711868002/terry-collins/)

USA TODAY

Updated May 6, 2026, 8:58 p.m. ET

* A recent poll found most Americans disapproved of President Trump's comments and posts related to religion.
* Nearly 9 in 10 Americans had a negative reaction to an AI-generated image of Trump as a Jesus Christ-like figure.
* The poll also found a majority of Americans disapproved of Trump's posts regarding the Iran conflict.
* Pope Leo XIV holds a 25-point net favorable margin among Americans, according to the poll.

Most Americans disapprove of President [Donald Trump](/news/politics/donald-trump/)'s comments about Pope Leo XIV. But they object even more to his recent AI image depicting him as Jesus Christ, a new poll shows.

Nearly 9 in 10 Americans (87%) have a negative reaction to the Trump image, which he posted on Truth Social, including 69% who express a strongly negative reaction, results from the latest [Washington Post-ABC News-Ipsos poll](https://www.washingtonpost.com/tablet/2026/04/30/april-24-28-2026-washington-post-abc-news-ipsos-poll/) show.

The latest findings from the [wide-ranging poll conducted April 24-28](/story/news/politics/2026/05/03/trumps-disapproval-rating-hits-record-high-poll/89920080007/) on Trump's handling of the Iran war, the economy, and the cost of living come as the president has [refueled angst](/story/news/politics/2026/05/06/trump-reignites-pope-feud/89958873007/) with the pope just days before Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](/news/politics/marco-rubio/) is scheduled to make a high-stakes visit to the Vatican.

The image posted on April 12 showed Trump in white and red robes, laying his hand on a sick man's forehead with a light emanating from his other hand, as an American flag waved and an American eagle flew in the background, which many interpreted as a Christ-like or messianic depiction.

The post [sparked widespread backlash](/story/news/politics/2026/04/13/trump-jesus-truth-social-backlash-pope-leo/89590926007/), even from some of Trump's party, and [allegations of blasphemy](/story/news/politics/2026/04/13/trump-jesus-truth-social-backlash-pope-leo/89590926007/). The post was deleted from the president's Truth Social account on April 13.

"I thought it was [me as a doctor](/story/news/trump/2026/04/13/trump-jesus-doctor-red-cross-image/89590847007/). And it had to do with Red Cross," Trump told reporters at the White House on April 13. "And only the fake news could come up with that one ... I make people a lot better."

The poll said 80% of those who voted for Trump and 79% of Republicans surveyed had a negative reaction to Trump’s post.

Shortly before the president posted the controversial image, he wrote [a lengthy message](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116394704213456431) on Truth Social criticizing the pope, who has been critical of the Iran war, as "Weak on Crime, Weak on Nuclear Weapons." The pontiff [responded](/story/news/politics/2026/04/12/donald-trump-pope-leo-xiv-iran-criticism/89584876007/) and said he has "no fear of the Trump administration, or speaking out loudly of the message of the Gospel."

Trump then doubled down on April 15, [resharing an AI-generated image](/story/news/politics/2026/04/15/donald-trump-jesus-image-pope/89629539007/) showing him being [embraced by Jesus](/story/news/politics/2026/04/13/donald-trump-picture-jesus-pope-leo/89586966007/). The post showed Trump with an angelic light and the U.S. flag circling him and the AI Jesus. 

"The Radical Left Lunatics might not like this, but I think it is quite nice!!! President [DJT](/news/politics/donald-trump/)," Trump wrote in the April 15 post, which had its own caption reading: "I was never a very religious man .. but doesn't it seem, with all these satanic, demonic, child sacrificing monsters being exposed ... that God might be playing his Trump card!"

## Americans respond negatively to Trump's controversial Iran post

The Washington Post-ABC-Ipsos poll also noted that a majority of Americans responded negatively to another controversial Truth Social post on April 7 about the Iran war that drew widespread condemnation.

The poll said 76% of Americans disapprove of Trump’s post threatening "[a whole civilization will die tonight, never to be brought back again,](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116363336033995961)" if Iran did not make a deal with the U.S., which seemingly suggested the use of nuclear weapons.

Another 53% of Americans polled "strongly" disapproving the post, which also led to extensive scrutiny and debate from fellow politicians, theologians, and other experts who study ethics and war.

Most Catholics (75%) and Protestants (70%) polled also had a negative reaction to Trump’s post, along with non-Christians (72%).

Additionally, 69% of Americans disapproved of Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth praying for U.S. troops to inflict "overwhelming violence of action against those who deserve no mercy," the poll found.

## Poll finds Pope favorable among Americans

The Washington Post-ABC-Ipsos poll found that many Americans favored the pontiff. The poll said Pope Leo has a 25-point net favorable margin among Americans overall, with 41% favorable, 16% unfavorable, and 43% saying they have no opinion of the pontiff.

The poll also noted that 66% of Americans have a positive reaction to Pope Leo asking Americans on April 7 to contact Congress to work for peace and reject war, while 57% polled have a negative reaction to Trump's Truth Social post that read, "I don’t want a Pope who thinks it’s OK for Iran to have a Nuclear Weapon." 

Among Catholic Americans, 70% had a positive reaction to Pope Leo asking Americans to contact Congress, and 61% were negative toward Trump’s post about the pope and nuclear weapons.

Overall, among Catholic Americans who have seen [this feud](/story/news/nation/2026/04/19/trump-feud-pope-leo-divides-catholics/89667533007/#:~:text=While%20observers%20say%20it%20might,Catholic%20right%20before%20the%20midterms.%E2%80%9D) become the most [contentious relationship](/story/news/nation/2026/04/16/timeline-escalating-feud-between-trump-and-pope-leo/89639868007/) between a secular leader and the papacy dating back to medieval times, the pope's favorability climbs to 61%, the poll said. Just 14% disapprove of the pope, and 25% of those Catholics polled have no opinion.

Meanwhile, [Trump’s approval rating](/story/news/politics/2026/05/03/trumps-disapproval-rating-hits-record-high-poll/89920080007/) has dropped to 37%, down from 45% in February 2025, the poll said.
