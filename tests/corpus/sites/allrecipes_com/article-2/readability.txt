My coworker Lori makes this brunch salad and we all absolutely love it! It's quick and easy with absolutely no cooking required. Great during the warm summer months.


 




Prep Time:
15 mins


Total Time:
15 mins


Servings:
8 







Keep Screen Awake

Ingredients
 





1/2x







1x







2x







Original recipe (1X) yields 8 servings

 

2 (10 ounce) bags baby spinach


2  bananas, sliced


12  fresh strawberries, sliced


1 (10 ounce) can mandarin oranges, drained


½ cup dried cranberries


½ cup pine nuts


1 cup poppy seed salad dressing



Directions


 Gather the ingredients.

 
 Greg DuPree/ Food Stylist: Ana Kelly / Prop Stylist: Prissy Montiel




 Place spinach in a large bowl. Arrange bananas, strawberries, mandarin oranges, cranberries, and pine nuts on top.

 
 Greg DuPree/ Food Stylist: Ana Kelly / Prop Stylist: Prissy Montiel




 Toss with poppy seed dressing before serving.
